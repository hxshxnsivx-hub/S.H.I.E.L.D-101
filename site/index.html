the idea

It sits on the tool call

An agent's real risk is the arguments it hands to a tool. Kavach reads every argument before it leaves, decides what's safe, and acts — while your model, prompt and graph stay exactly as they are.

3

Decide

Allow, mask, tokenize, block or escalate — by destination trust.

4

Prove

A tamper-evident audit and a one-click DPDP report.
