How it works

Sits on the tool call. Decides. Proves.

An agent's real risk is the arguments it passes to a tool. Kavach intercepts every argument before dispatch, classifies it, and acts — while the model, the prompt and the graph stay exactly as they were.

01

Attach

One wrapper — SDK, LangChain, MCP, OpenAI, or voice STT/TTS.

02

Detect

Verhoeff and Luhn checksums for Aadhaar and card, plus Indic NER — even spoken number-words.

03

Decide

Allow, mask, tokenize, block or escalate, by destination trust and sensitivity.

04

Prove

A hash-chained audit log and a one-click DPDP evidence report.

Architecture

Detect, decide, act — then prove it

Deterministic checks run first — Verhoeff, Luhn, PAN, IFSC, GSTIN and a UPI allowlist — then a multilingual NER model catches the names and health terms the rules miss. Each finding is scored by sensitivity, matched against the destination's trust, and acted on. Secrets never egress, whatever the destination.

guard your agent — one line
from kavach import Kavach, Trust
from kavach.integrations.langchain import guard_tools

kav = Kavach(trust={"crm_api": Trust.UNTRUSTED})
agent = create_react_agent(llm, guard_tools(kav, tools))  # guarded
Why Kavach

Built for India's data, and its rules

Western DLP tools were trained on Western identifiers. Kavach is built from the other direction — Indian PII, Indian languages and the DPDP Act.

India-native

The identifiers and scripts that generic tools miss, caught by checksum and model together.
